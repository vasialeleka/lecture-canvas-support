Lecture Canvas — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Lecture Canvas is a note-taking whiteboard for iPad and iPhone. It is designed to keep your notes private.

What we collect

Nothing. Lecture Canvas does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests.

Where your notes are stored

Your notes, drawings, images and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. Images you add from Photos or paste from the clipboard are copied into that storage. They may be included in your own device backups (iCloud or computer backup) according to your device settings. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Photos and clipboard

When you insert a photo, you choose it with the system photo picker; the app receives only the photo you pick. Pasting uses the system Paste button or ⌘V and only reads the clipboard when you ask it to.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
