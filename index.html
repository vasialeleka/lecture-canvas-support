Lecture Canvas — Support

An infinite whiteboard for lecture notes on iPad and iPhone.

Get in touch

Questions, bug reports and feature requests are all welcome: vasialeleka@ukr.net.

It helps if you say which device and iOS version you are on, and what you did just before the problem appeared. Replies usually come within a few days.

Common questions

Does it work without Apple Pencil?
Yes. Turn on Draw with Finger in the ⋯ menu of a note and you can write with your finger. With Apple Pencil, one finger pans instead of drawing.
How do I move around a note?
One finger pans, two fingers pan and zoom. Home goes back to where the note started and Fit All shows everything at once; both are at the bottom right. Bookmarks jump to a saved place.
How do I erase just part of a stroke?
Pick the eraser, then tap it again to choose between erasing part of a stroke and erasing whole strokes, and to set its size. A double tap on Apple Pencil switches to the eraser and back.
Can I paste something copied on my Mac?
Yes. Copy an image or text on the Mac and use the Paste button at the top right, or ⌘V with a keyboard. Universal Clipboard needs both devices signed in to the same Apple Account with Bluetooth, Wi‑Fi and Handoff on.
Where are my notes kept, and are they backed up?
On your device only, inside the app's own storage. They are included in your own iCloud or computer device backup. There is no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes its notes.
Can I get my notes out?
Not yet. Export is the feature most likely to come next — tell me what format would help you.
